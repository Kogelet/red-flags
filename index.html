How to play ▾
  1. On your turn you're dating someone new. Draw a red flag.
  2. Tell the story out loud: who they are, how you found out, what happened.
  3. Decide: STAY ❤️ or BREAK UP 💔.
  4. Stay, and your next red flag belongs to the same partner — connect them into one ridiculous story.
  5. Break up, and next turn you start fresh with someone new.
  6. When the red flags run out: who's still together, and who survived the most? 😂
