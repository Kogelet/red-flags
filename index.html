📜 How to play
  1. Everyone writes funny, weird or outrageous red flags (e.g. “Calls his mother during every date.”). They all go into the hat.
  2. On your turn you're dating an imaginary person. Draw a red flag.
  3. Invent the story out loud: who you're dating, how you discovered the red flag, what happened.
  4. Decide: STAY ❤️ or BREAK UP 💔.
  5. If you stay, your next red flag belongs to the same partner — connect them all into one ridiculous story.
  6. If you break up, next turn you start dating someone new from zero.
  7. Keep going around the circle until the hat is empty. Then see who's still together — and who survived the most red flags. 😂
